Biography in English

Hongyok (Hongyok Khuanpradit) is a 4th-generation CGM48 member who joined as a trainee on 22 March 2025. She was born on 27 January 2004 in Rayong.

She was in the A-side senbatsu for the 11th single, Koi Tsunjatta.
