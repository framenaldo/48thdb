Biography in English

Nall (Nalliya Wipakkit) is a 5th-generation BNK48 member who joined as a trainee in February 2024. She was born on 25 August 2001 in Chiang Mai, and is the oldest of her generation and of BNK48 today.

She placed 28th, in the Next Girls, at the 5th general election. Her pastimes are long baths, Thai massage, swimming, games and face masks, and she loves cherries, snow, anime, manhwa, make-up, idols and dry suki with omelette. #Gamer #Anime
