Biography in English

Janry (Kalnyarat Panphiphat) is a 4th-generation BNK48 member. She joined as a trainee in October 2022 and was promoted to #TeamBIII in September 2024. She was born on 3 January 2008 in Bangkok, and her Chinese name is 钟娇乐.

She is the #Centre of the 20th single, Masaka no Confession, and of the song Shoujotachi yo – วันใหม่ on Iiwake Maybe. She has been in six A-side senbatsu and placed 16th, in the Under Girls, at the 5th general election. She is best friends with Emmy, and loves series, food, sleep and Sanrio. Her light-stick colours are green and white.
