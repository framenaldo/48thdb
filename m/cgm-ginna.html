Biography in English

Ginna (Munchupa Moonklang) is a 2nd-generation CGM48 member. She joined in October 2022 and was promoted to #TeamC in May 2026. She was born on 10 December 2006 in Chiang Mai.

She was the last of her generation to be chosen for a senbatsu, appearing in the A-side senbatsu for ได้(ด้าย)ไหม.
