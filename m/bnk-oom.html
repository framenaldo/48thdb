Biography in English

Oom (Natcha Krisdhasima) is a former 2nd-generation BNK48 member who joined as a trainee in April 2018. She was born on 29 September 2002.

She placed 27th, in the Under Girls, at the first general election, was in the A-side senbatsu for 77 no Suteki na Machi e, and graduated on 16 February 2020.
