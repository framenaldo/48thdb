Biography in English

Jan (Jetsupa Kruetang) is a former 1st-generation BNK48 member. She joined in December 2016 and became a member of #TeamBIII in December 2017. She was born on 8 April 1994.

She was in the A-side senbatsu for the group's first two singles, อยากจะได้พบเธอ and Koisuru Fortune Cookie, and graduated on 28 February 2018.
