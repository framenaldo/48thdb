Biography in English

Rose (Panisara Buranapha) is a 6th-generation BNK48 member who joined as a trainee in May 2025. She was born on 1 November 2007 in Nakhon Pathom.

She topped the poll for the Thai–Japanese special project and became the #Centre of Kimi ga Iru Dake de.

She likes series, games, shopping and writing stories, loves cats, dogs and rabbits, and her favourites are Japanese food, barbecue and som tam. Her MBTI is INFP. #Gamer
