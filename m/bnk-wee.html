Biography in English

Wee (Weeraya Zhang) is a former 2nd-generation BNK48 member. She joined as a trainee in April 2018 and was in #TeamBIII from November 2019 until she graduated on 30 April 2024. She was born on 23 October 2001.

She made the Senbatsu at all four general elections she stood in — 13th, 8th, 11th and 8th — and was in eleven A-side senbatsu, from BNK Festival to Kiss Me! (ให้ฉันได้รู้).
