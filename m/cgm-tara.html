Biography in English

Thara Korkiatmanon is a member of CGM48, 5th generation, and the youngest member of the generation. She joined the group as a trainee on 7 February 2026. Born on 5 June 2013 in Chiang Mai, she enjoys reading books, watching movies, practicing yoga, and pilates.

Thara was scouted from a ball house, where she was invited to apply for CGM48's 5th generation before deciding to apply and becoming a member of the 5th generation to this day.
