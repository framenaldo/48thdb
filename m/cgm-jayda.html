Biography in English

Jayda (Ravinda Alan) is a former 1st-generation CGM48 member who joined as a trainee in September 2019. She was born on 16 December 2006.

She shares the #Centre of Chain of love on มะลิ with Fortune, was in the A-side senbatsu for มะลิ and MAESHIKA MUKANEE, placed 40th, in the Next Girls, at the 3rd general election, and graduated on 7 October 2023.
